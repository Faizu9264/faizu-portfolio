Hours a week
How long you spend on this task now.
Value
How much you gain if AI does it. More hours saved or more customers won = a higher number. It fills in from your hours; raise it if the task brings in customers.
Feasibility
How easy it is to hand over today. Is the information already written down (price list, FAQ)? Would you spot a mistake before the customer does? Two yeses = 4 or 5. Two nos = 1 or 2.

1 is low, 5 is high. The best first task is high on both: a big gain that is easy to start.

    Up = more value (bigger gain). Right = more feasible (easier to start). The top right corner is where your first AI task comes from.

    How the score works

    Score = Value × Feasibility, out of 25. Multiplying means a task must be both worth doing and easy to hand over to reach the top. Value comes from hours saved: 5+ hours a week = 5, 3–4 = 4, 2 = 3, 1 = 2, under 1 = 1. On a tie, the task with more hours wins. Keep the next two for later.
