Two prompts from the session

Poster prompt

[Product] on [surface or setting], soft morning window light, clean product photo for Instagram, brand colours [x and y], empty space at the top for the text '[offer]'. Square format.

Replace the words in brackets. Start from your own product photo and check the spelling on the poster.

Lead-sorting prompt

Label this message HOT, QUESTION or COMPLAINT. Reply with the label only. If unsure, reply QUESTION. Message: [paste the customer's message]

This one sentence is the whole AI step in the form → sheet → alert workflow.

BEFORE YOU AUTOMATE

The rules that keep your accounts safe

  • Check what reaches customers.Prices, dates, promises, payments and refunds get a human check.
  • Instagram and Facebook.Automation may only reply to people who commented or messaged first. Use Meta-approved apps that never ask for your password.
  • LinkedIn.Let AI write. Do not use bots that connect, message or like for you.
  • WhatsApp.The customer writes first. Never use unofficial bulk-sender tools: they get numbers banned.
  • YouTube.AI is allowed. Mass-produced, repetitive videos do not earn. Keep yourself in the video.
